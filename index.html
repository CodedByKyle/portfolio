About

Helping people and being of service is one of the foundations my life is built on. That, together with a lifelong interest in technology, is why I love to build things that make the world a better place.

From 2017 to 2019 I worked at VCA Emergency Animal Care Clinic, assisting in surgeries and monitoring anesthesia. In 2022 I joined the Milo Foundation, a non-profit, no-kill animal rescue and sanctuary. At their Point Richmond facility, where I still work, I built the first standalone medical department and moved it onto digital systems, including software and systems I taught myself to design. I also do freelance consulting in software development, graphic design, 3D modeling and brand solutions.

I only recently started studying computer science and electronics engineering formally, but I've been taking technology apart my whole life. When I'm not at work you'll likely find me in my "lab": 3D modeling and printing parts, wiring PCBs, pulling apart electronics, or writing the software for whatever I'm building next.

I'm looking for a software or solution engineering role where what I build helps people at a scale I can't reach on my own.
